Why some names cannot simply be the team colour

This project holds a 4.5:1 contrast floor for text. Measured against white, … of 32 primaries fail it outright and two more sit within a rounding error. Variant D therefore does not use the raw hex — it darkens each colour just far enough to pass, which keeps the team recognisable without breaking the floor.
